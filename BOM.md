Bill of Materials (BOM)
​Summary & Cost Breakdown
​The ESP32 Local Storage Server hardware build costs approximately $11.80 and consists of an ESP32-WROOM-32 board ($3.50), a MicroSD Card SPI Module ($1.00), a 16GB/32GB MicroSD Card ($4.00), power-rail decoupling capacitors including a 10µF electrolytic ($0.10) and a 0.1µF ceramic ($0.05), a 3mm activity LED ($0.10) paired with a 330Ω resistor ($0.05), a Micro-USB/USB-C power cable ($1.50), and a prototype PCB board ($1.50).
​For the setup and development environment, a Raspberry Pi 5 (8GB RAM) costing approximately $80.00 (MSRP) is included as the host workstation for writing, compiling, and flashing firmware to the ESP32 via USB. This brings the overall project development budget to ~$91.80.
